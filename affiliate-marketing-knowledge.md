=======================================================
# - Jald se jald 3 sales lane ke liye strategies
=======================================================
1. Home Office & Desk Setup (Aapke niche se milti-julti)
Log apne workspace ko sundar banane ke liye choti-moti cheezein aksar kharidte hain. Inmein conversion rate achha hota hai.

Products: RGB Mousepads, Laptop stands, Cable organizers, Desk lamps, ya Mechanical keyboard keycaps.

Kyun: Inki keemat ₹300 - ₹1,000 ke beech hoti hai. Log inke liye lambe reviews nahi padhte, sirf photo dekh kar pasand kar lete hain.

2. Mobile & Tech Accessories
Yeh sabse fast bikne wali category hai kyunki har kisi ko inki zarurat hoti hai.

Products: Fast charging cables, Screen protectors, Mobile holders (car/bike), ya TWS earbuds (budget wale).

Kyun: Inki demand kabhi khatam nahi hoti. Agar aap ek "Best Budget Accessories under ₹500" jaisa blog post likhte hain, toh sales aane ke chances bahut zyada hain.

3. Kitchen & Home Utilities (High Volume)
Yeh category un logo ke liye best hai jo social media (Instagram/Facebook) se traffic lana chahte hain.

Products: Vegetable choppers, Rechargeable water dispensers, Mini vacuum sealers, ya Silicone lids.

Kyun: In products ke video jaldi viral hote hain. Agar aap ek chota sa video bana kar apni site ka link dein, toh 3 sales ek din mein bhi ho sakti hain.

4. Self-Care & Personal Grooming
Products: Face massagers, Beard combs, Electric toothbrushes (entry-level), ya Essential oil diffusers.

Kyun: Ye products "Gifting" ke liye bahut popular hain.
